I am a quantum information researcher at University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). After earning my Ph.D. from USTC (2017) and postdoctoral work at USTC (2017-2018), SUSTech (2018-2020), and again USTC (2020-2022), I became a Research Associate focusing on how information behaves in quantum systems, spanning quantum foundations, quantum cryptography, and information thermodynamics.
