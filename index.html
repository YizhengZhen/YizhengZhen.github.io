Yi-Zheng Zhen

甄一政

Associate Researcher · University of Science and Technology of China

I work at the intersection of quantum information theory, thermodynamics, and experimental quantum physics — exploring how information is governed by fundamental physical constraints, and how those constraints shape the technologies of tomorrow.

Research Interests

Information Thermodynamics
  • Finite‑time nonequilibrium thermodynamics
  • Work/heat of (quantum) information operations
  • Energy consumption of quantum operations or algorithms
Quantum Foundations
  • Measures of Bell nonlocality and entanglement
  • Energy constraints of entanglement distribution
Quantum Communication
  • Device‑independent quantum protocols
  • Entropy accumulation theorem
  • Quantum repeaters
  • Entanglement purification and distillation
  • Quantum network configuration
Hybrid Quantum Systems
  • Superconducting circuits and spin ensembles
  • Superradiance
  • Disordered / inhomogeneous Tavis–Cummings models
  • Quantum transducers
