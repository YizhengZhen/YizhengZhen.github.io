      <strong>Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution Based on the Mermin-Peres Magic Square Game</strong>
    </div>
    <div class="work-card-body">
      <dl class="work-card-meta">
        <dt>Authors:</dt>
        <dd>Y.-Z. Zhen, Y. Mao, Y.-Z. Zhang, F. Xu, and B. C. Sanders</dd>
        <dt>Venue:</dt>
        <dd><em>Phys. Rev. Lett.</em> <strong>131</strong>, 080801 (2023) <a href="https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.080801">→ Read Paper</a></dd>
        <dt>Summary:</dt>
        <dd>Proposes a DIQKD protocol based on the Mermin-Peres magic square game, a "pseudotelepathy" nonlocal game. The protocol achieves a higher key rate than standard CHSH-based DIQKD and reveals the cryptographic advantage of quantum pseudotelepathy.</dd>
      </dl>

    <div class="work-card-title">
      <span class="work-card-icon">♨️</span>
      <strong>Universal Bound on Energy Cost of Bit Reset in Finite Time</strong>
    </div>
    <div class="work-card-body">
      <dl class="work-card-meta">
        <dt>Authors:</dt>
        <dd>Y.-Z. Zhen, D. Egloff, K. Modi, and O. Dahlsten</dd>
        <dt>Venue:</dt>
        <dd><em>Phys. Rev. Lett.</em> <strong>127</strong>, 190602 (2021) <a href="https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.127.190602">→ Read Paper</a></dd>
        <dt>Summary:</dt>
        <dd>Establishes a universal trade-off between the energy cost and the time required for bit reset. Shows that the work penalty scales inversely with time for fast operations, bridging the gap between Landauer's principle and finite-time thermodynamics.</dd>
      </dl>
    </div>
  </div>
